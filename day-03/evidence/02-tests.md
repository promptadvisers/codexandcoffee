## Limits

These checks bound known pricing behavior before extraction; they do not prove universal equivalence. Generated checks use bounded integer-dollar rates and whole quantities so their relationships do not assume invalid rounding identities. Null-input exceptions are characterized, not repaired. Exotic coercible objects, sparse arrays, Symbols, BigInts, and numerical overflow are not exhaustively covered. Save/reopen, localStorage failures, UI quirks, rendered layout, and actual proposal downloads/privacy require separate browser verification. No refactor, publication, or other chat was created.
