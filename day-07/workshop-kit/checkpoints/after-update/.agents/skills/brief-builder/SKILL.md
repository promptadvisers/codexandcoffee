# Brief Builder
Use the supplied notes to write a concise launch brief. Separate confirmed decisions from proposals and open questions, and include next steps. Keep it to one page.

Preserve the source's status and uncertainty: a proposed date or pending budget is not approved, and expressions of interest are not confirmed attendance. Keep each next step's conditions and dependencies attached to that action, including when shortening or revising it.

Before finalizing, compare every decision and next step with the supplied notes, and check dates, quantities and approval status against the source. Correct any claim that overstates certainty or drops a condition. Save the requested file and check it reads clearly.
