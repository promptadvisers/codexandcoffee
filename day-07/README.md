# Day 7: Better Skills with Retro

A self-contained, 45-minute workshop on turning repeated corrections into useful skills. No earlier lesson or project is required.

- **[Download the PowerPoint](https://github.com/promptadvisers/codexandcoffee/raw/refs/heads/main/day-07/Day-07.pptx)** — 32 slides with the Codex & Coffee cover, demonstrations, practice and speaker notes
- **[Download the learner kit](https://github.com/promptadvisers/codexandcoffee/raw/refs/heads/main/day-07/Retro-workshop-kit.zip)** — the skills, fictional session, exact prompts and a verified after-update checkpoint
- [Browse the kit and setup instructions](workshop-kit/START-HERE.md)

## What we do

Start with a fictional launch-brief conversation in which important qualifications keep getting lost. Review the session, focus the review on one recurring problem, then inspect the skill that guides the work. Retro Electro extends the review into a proposed or authorized workspace update. Finish with a fresh task that checks the updated instructions.

## The skills

- **Retro** reviews a completed session and proposes improvements without editing files
- **Retro Electro** previews or applies scoped workspace changes when requested
- **Brief Builder** is the small sample skill used in the demonstrations

In Codex, invoke a skill with `$retro` or `$retro-electro`, followed by your request. The `mode=preview` and `mode=apply` examples are conventions defined by these skills, not built-in Codex flags. The [official skills guide](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills) describes setup and discovery.

Unzip the kit and open `practice-workspace` as a local Codex project. The hidden `.agents/skills` folder is included. Read the kit's START-HERE before the apply exercise: protected skill folders may need a narrowly scoped write permission. The supplied after-update checkpoint lets you continue without changing global access settings.

All names, dates and conversations in the exercises are fictional. The examples are not connected to live accounts, and the kit does not send or publish anything. Trials used native Codex CLI sessions; results do not guarantee behavior in every client or future run.
