The board now opens with a GPT Image illustration of a phased plan: understand the job, choose a small first version, build one working piece, check it, and hand it over. It also shows the goal, inputs, small steps, completion check and pause condition inside one phase.

## Try one example

1. Download and unzip the Day 4 package
2. Open `Day-04.excalidraw` in [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com): top-left menu → Open
3. Start with the illustration at the top, then zoom into any example
4. Open a copy of that example's folder in Codex and paste its `PROMPT.txt`
5. Review the proposed first version, assumptions and checks before asking Codex to build

You can also view the [opening illustration](images/anatomy-of-a-phased-plan.png) and [section previews](board-previews/) without importing the board.

## How to read the board

Blue: the situation. Purple: the source material. Amber: the first useful version. Coral: what stays out. Teal: the plan and checks to return. The notes explain the job of each paragraph; they are not part of the copyable prompt.
