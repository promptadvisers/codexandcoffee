# Day 15 · Your One-Person Army

A standalone 45-minute lesson with 51 slides and 180 practical uses for Codex. Explore 30 ideas each for sales, marketing, finance, operations, HR and recruitment, then adapt the prompts to a bottleneck in your own work.

Extract the learner kit and open START-HERE.md. Copy workspace-template into your own project, fill in your context and systems, and choose a repeated job from the catalog. The prompts walk through identifying an opportunity, researching your systems, building a small first version, and making a successful method reusable.

## What the lesson covers

- An AI workspace with AGENTS.md, skills, conventions, context, prepared inputs and outputs
- On-demand work, scheduled reviews and connected workflows
- Starting model choices for writing, visual work, extraction and workflow construction
- A Decisions API routing pattern with a review route for uncertain results
- Marketing assets including images, explainers, video edits and consistent campaign material

The catalog supplies ideas and adaptable prompts. Test the selected workflow with your actual sources and available system access. The kit includes a blank workspace template and creates no live integrations or scheduled jobs. HR and recruitment examples support the process while responsible people make employment decisions.
